Nutrition and dietary support services
Source: https://dining.columbia.edu/content/nutrition

Summary of official Columbia Dining information.

Columbia Dining describes individual consultations for concerns including food allergies, intolerances, vegetarian or vegan eating, and weight management. Customized meal planning is available for students with allergies or other restrictive dietary needs.

Students can contact Dining about individual or group tours for guidance in the dining halls. The nutrition page links to an Eat Well guide and meal-building examples for John Jay, Ferris Booth Commons, and JJ's Place.
