Dining Dollars, Flex, and dining-plan basics
Source: https://dining.columbia.edu/content/dining-plans

Summary of official Columbia Dining information.

Dining plans provide an allowance of meals counted weekly or by term. Benefits differ by plan.

Dining Dollars and Flex are declining-balance accounts accessed with a Columbia University ID. Dining Dollars are available to enrolled Columbia students and are tax-free. Flex is available to enrolled students, faculty, and staff and is not tax-free.

Both can pay at on-campus dining locations. Flex additionally supports purchases at the University Bookstore, campus copy and vending machines, and food delivery through Grubhub. Both balances carry forward between years until graduation. The page directs users to SSOL to obtain or fund these accounts.
