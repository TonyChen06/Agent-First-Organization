Dietary labels and food-allergy support
Source: https://dining.columbia.edu/content/food-allergies-special-diets

Summary of official Columbia Dining information.

Columbia's menu key uses GF for gluten-free, V for vegetarian, VN for vegan, and H for halal. Its descriptions state that GF items exclude gluten-containing ingredients and H items contain only halal ingredients.

Columbia describes gluten-free and halal stations at John Jay, vegan and vegetarian options across its locations, and kosher grab-and-go food at John Jay.

Columbia explicitly identifies a risk of allergen cross-contact in its dining halls. Students should check station allergen information and ask a manager to review ingredients when needed. Missing allergen information is not evidence that a food is safe.

The registered dietitian offers individual consultation for personalized allergy-management plans. Use the contact links on the official page to reach Dining.
