Dining-plan frequently asked questions
Source: https://dining.columbia.edu/content/frequently-asked-questions

Summary of official Columbia Dining information.

A separate dining card is unnecessary: meal plans, Dining Dollars, and Flex are accessed through the Columbia University ID.

Location access depends on the student's plan category. Guest meals are available, but the number depends on the plan; tell the cashier when using meals for guests.

Weekly meal allowances expire on Saturday night and refresh on Sunday morning. Term-based meals expire at the academic year's end. Unused fall term meals may carry into spring, but those carried meals become available after the spring allocation is exhausted and are not shown in SSOL.

Plan changes are limited to the designated change period.
