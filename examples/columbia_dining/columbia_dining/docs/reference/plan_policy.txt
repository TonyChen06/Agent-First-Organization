Dining-plan policy: enrollment, access, and takeout
Source: https://dining.columbia.edu/content/dining-plan-policy

Summary of official Columbia Dining information.

Columbia states that dining-plan enrollment covers an academic year. Plan options can be changed during the specified change periods; plans cannot be cancelled, shared, or converted into Flex or Dining Dollars.

Access varies with student affiliation, including distinctions for graduate students and Teachers College students.

One meal swipe covers one meal. The policy describes a reusable takeout-container program and identifies John Jay, JJ's Place, and Ferris Booth as participating residential locations.
